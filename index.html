Questions owners ask

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not. Most businesses already pay for AI inside Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace without using it. We start with what you have.

Is our client data safe?

Everything is built in your own accounts, and you get clear rules about what can and can't go into AI tools. I don't keep copies of your data.

What if my team won't use it?

That's the part I'm best at. Training is part of every offer, built around your team's real work, and I check in after a week.

What does "working as agreed" mean?

Before I start, we write down in a paragraph exactly what your Quick Win will do. If it isn't doing that within 7 days, you don't pay.

Who actually does the work?

I do. No account managers and no hand-offs: you deal with the person building it.

Do you work outside Adelaide?

Yes. Everything works by video call, anywhere in Australia. If you're in the Hills or Adelaide, we can also meet in person.
